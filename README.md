# Dr Jamie Pike

Jamie is a bioinformatician leading a Knowledge Transfer Partnership between the Earlham Institute and Tozer Seeds. He leverages genomics to accelerate vegetable breeding and deliver new varieties for commercial growers.

Jamie earned his PhD at the University of Warwick (Waitrose Collaborative Training Partnership), investigating pathogen diversity and developing diagnostic tools for Fusarium wilt. Having worked at Rothamsted Research and the Eden Project, he now focuses on translating academic research into industrial applications to bolster British food security.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Many of my repositories are private due to ongoing commercial breeding partnerships. I am happy to discuss my methodologies and any non-commercially sensitive code.
